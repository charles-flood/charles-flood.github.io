About Me

A painter and decorator/cleaning technitian in a past life I pivoted to the tech industry in the mid 2010s. Starting out as an intern in hostelworld then moving onto software engineering at iQuate. I learned quickly that I had a knack for coding and enjoyed solving problems others couldn't or didn't want to take on. I gravitated towards backend and database coding/scripting. Data integrity and fast retrieval both something I enjoyed working on. From combating little/big endian through cross platform virtualization and pulling dlls in house to manage password prompts over protocols to utilising mssql system level tables for lightning quick number manipulation. There are solutions to be found for every problem. While initially I was not interested in moving into a management role, I have a tendency to speak my mind and assist those around me so I landed in a scrum master role. I thoroughly enjoyed this role and found that one of the more important aspects was to protect the team from unecessary external pressure and requests. Once all scrum ceremonies were locked in of course. With the company pivoting to cloud tech I ended up taking ownership of the on-premises software and moved into the software developement manager role. This gave me a lot of freedom to steer the future direction of the software and remove a lot of existing chaff/bloat. Wearing a few hats (scrum master, dev lead, product owner, dev manager) as it was a small team, we retained all existing contracts and streamlined a lot of the back end. Unforetunately funding dried up and the company closed resulting in redundancies. I've taken a break since then to work on some personal projects but now looking to move onto new challenges. Outside of work I'm a big fan of rugby both provincial and international. Music is a huge passion of mine and love getting out to gigs when I can. Im also an avid gamer both online and off, competitive and casual. And when I'm not doing any of these, I occasionally subject myself to the pain of golf, with Corballis being my home club.
